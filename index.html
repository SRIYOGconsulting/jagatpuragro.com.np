Our Boer Goat Breeding Philosophy

We believe quality livestock production starts with quality genetics and proper management.

Our breeding program emphasizes:

  • Purebred Boer genetics
  • Systematic breeding
  • Pedigree-based breeding
  • Animal identification
  • Weight monitoring
  • Health management
  • Vaccination records
  • Breeding records

We aim to make quality breeding resources accessible to farmers and livestock entrepreneurs across Nepal.
