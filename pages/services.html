Our Services

We provide integrated agricultural and livestock services focused on quality breeding, feed production, farmer collaboration and sustainable agricultural practices.

01

Boer Goat Breeding

We specialize in Boer goat breeding with a focus on quality genetics, systematic breeding and proper livestock management.

Our breeding program originated from 113 purebred Boer goats imported from South Africa and continues to focus on developing quality breeding stock.

02

Purebred Boer Goats

We develop and maintain purebred Boer goats for farmers, commercial livestock farms and breeding programs.

Our focus includes proper identification, health monitoring, breeding records and pedigree-based management.

03

Improved Boer Goat Breeding

Our breeding program can support the development of improved goats through planned breeding and quality breeding stock.

The objective is to combine desirable Boer characteristics with suitable livestock production systems in Nepal.

04

Silage Production

We produce quality silage as a livestock feed resource and work with farmers to connect maize production with silage production.

Our process includes maize cultivation, harvesting, chopping, processing, preservation and storage.

05

Maize Production Collaboration

We collaborate with farmers in maize cultivation by supporting the production process and connecting harvested maize with silage production.

This creates an opportunity for farmers to generate additional value from maize production.

06

Organic Fertilizer

We work toward producing organic fertilizer using livestock manure and other organic resources.

Cow dung and goat manure can be processed, matured and utilized as agricultural fertilizer, supporting the recycling of nutrients from livestock farming back into the soil.

07

Farmer Collaboration

We work with farmers, livestock entrepreneurs and agricultural stakeholders to develop integrated production systems.

Our approach focuses on creating practical connections between farming, livestock, feed and agricultural resources.

08

Livestock Farming Support

We promote better livestock management through proper nutrition, breeding records, health management and planned production practices.

Our objective is to help farmers build productive and sustainable livestock enterprises.
