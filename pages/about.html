About Us

Jagatpur Agro Pvt. Ltd. is an agriculture and livestock enterprise based in Bharatpur, Chitwan, Nepal.

Our journey began with a commitment to improving Boer goat farming in Nepal. Starting in B.S. 2070/71, we began our Boer goat farming activities and, in B.S. 2072, imported 113 purebred Boer goats from South Africa to establish a systematic breeding program.

This foundation became the beginning of our “Boer Kranti” initiative.

Over the years, our focus has expanded beyond goat breeding. We are developing an integrated agricultural model that combines livestock production, maize cultivation, silage production and organic fertilizer.

Our approach is based on a simple principle: livestock farming and agriculture can support each other.

Maize can become silage for livestock. Livestock manure can become organic fertilizer for agricultural fields. Quality breeding genetics can improve livestock productivity. Farmers can participate throughout this interconnected production cycle.

Our Integrated Agriculture Model

  1. Maize
  2. Silage
  3. Livestock
  4. Manure
  5. Organic Fertilizer
  6. Agricultural Soil
  7. Crop Production
  8. Livestock Feed

This approach helps us work toward a more efficient and sustainable agricultural system.
