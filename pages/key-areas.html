Boer Kranti

Pure Genetics. Systematic Breeding. Better Livestock.

"Boer Kranti" is the distinctive Boer goat breeding initiative of Jagatpur Agro.

Our Boer breeding journey began with 113 purebred Boer goats imported from South Africa in B.S. 2072. Building on this foundation, we have continued a systematic breeding program focused on maintaining quality genetics and developing superior breeding stock.

Our management system emphasizes proper identification and record keeping, including:

  • Date of birth
  • Tag number
  • Weight
  • Health records
  • Vaccination records
  • Breeding history
  • Pedigree information

Our objective is to provide farmers and commercial livestock entrepreneurs with reliable breeding resources that can contribute to productive and sustainable goat farming.

Silage

Scientific Feed Solutions for Modern Livestock Farming

Good livestock production depends on good nutrition.

Jagatpur Agro works with farmers to connect maize cultivation with scientific silage production. Maize can be harvested at the appropriate stage, processed and safely preserved as silage, providing a valuable feed resource for livestock.

Our silage system connects:

  1. Seed
  2. Maize Production
  3. Harvesting
  4. Chopping
  5. Silage Production
  6. Safe Storage
  7. Animal Feed

Silage can help farmers manage livestock feed throughout the year, particularly during periods when fresh green fodder is less available.

Benefits include:

  • Year-round feed management
  • Better utilization of maize production
  • Reliable green-feed alternatives
  • Long-term feed preservation
  • Improved farm feed planning
  • Support for commercial livestock farms

Organic Fertilizer

From Livestock Farming to Soil Nutrition

At Jagatpur Agro, we believe agricultural resources should not go to waste.

Cow dung and goat manure generated through livestock farming can be processed and returned to agriculture as valuable organic resources.

Our organic fertilizer concept follows a circular system:

  1. Cow Dung + Goat Manure + Organic Resources
  2. Processing
  3. Maturation / Drying
  4. Organic Fertilizer
  5. Agricultural Fields

This approach connects livestock farming with crop production while supporting better utilization of organic materials.

Our goal is to contribute to:

  • Improved soil organic matter
  • Better soil structure
  • Nutrient management
  • Sustainable agricultural practices
  • Productive utilization of livestock waste
  • Circular agriculture
